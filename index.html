Water bill estimator

Compare your current monthly water charges with the rates proposed for January 1, 2027 through 2031. This is an estimate, not your bill.

The current estimate includes the Phase 3 drought surcharge of $0.45 per HCF now in effect by City Council action. Proposed rates for 2027–2031 do not include a drought surcharge.

The Water Adjustment Charge (WAC) and the utility user tax are not included. Your actual bill will differ.

Find these items on page 3 of your bill

Meter size, customer class, and usage are in the water row of the Account Details table. GWP bills are usually for about 60 days. This tool estimates a monthly water charge, so convert usage as shown in step 3. Pump zone is not listed on the bill; use the map farther down.

  1. 1

    Rate → customer class and meter size

    On this sample, WSF625 means Single Family and a 5/8-inch meter. The letters after W are the class (SF = Single Family). Match the number on your rate code to the meter size in the dropdown: 625 = 5/8-inch, 75 = 3/4-inch, 1.5 = 1 1/2-inch.

  2. 2

    Days → billing period

    This sample is 60 days (about two months). The calculator is monthly. If Days is about 60, divide usage by 2. If Days is about 30, enter usage as printed.

  3. 3

    Usage (HCF)

    This sample shows 60.51 HCF for 60 days, so enter about 30.26 as monthly usage (60.51 ÷ 2). 1 HCF = 748 gallons. Do not enter the Water Adjustment Charge, drought charge, taxes, electric, rubbish, or sewer into this tool.
